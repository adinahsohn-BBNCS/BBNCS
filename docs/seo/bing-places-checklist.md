## How Bing works for BBNCS

- Listing is tied to **Google Business Profile** — edit Google first, not Bing directly (unless Google is unavailable).
- Bing does **not** pull changes automatically. After GBP updates, open [bingplaces.com](https://www.bingplaces.com) → your listing → **Sync with Google**.
- Allow **24–72 hours** after sync, then spot-check Bing Maps: `Bits Bytes Nibbles Temecula`.
